The story so far...

Hey there, I'm Brandon. Thanks for stopping by! I'm a product designer with 12 years of experience and a background in advertising and visual design. Today, I lead product design end-to-end at Flipp, helping cross-functional teams turn complex user problems into thoughtful, shipped experiences.

Along the way, I've designed mobile and SaaS products, built design systems, mentored designers, and scaled design operations across multiple products and teams. My approach combines deep UX research, strong visual craft, and modern AI-driven workflows to rapidly prototype, explore complex interactions, and bridge the gap between design and front-end code.

Whether I'm running usability studies, refining component libraries, or partnering with engineering on implementation. I care about building engaging, intuitive products grounded in research and crafted with intention. If anything here resonates, I'd love to talk.
